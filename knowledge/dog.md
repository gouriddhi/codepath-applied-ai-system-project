# Adult Dog Care Guidelines

## Daily exercise
- Most adult dogs need 30–60 minutes of walking per day, split into two walks (morning and evening).
- High-energy breeds (herding, sporting, terriers) may need up to 90 minutes, including play or running.
- Add 10–15 minutes of enrichment daily: puzzle feeders, sniff walks, or short training sessions.

## Feeding
- Feed adult dogs twice a day, roughly 8–12 hours apart, at consistent times.
- Measure portions using the food label as a starting point, then adjust for body condition.
- Fresh water should always be available; refill and rinse the bowl daily.
- Avoid exercise for about 30 minutes before and after large meals, especially in large, deep-chested breeds.

## Grooming
- Brush short-coated dogs weekly and long or double-coated dogs 3–4 times per week (10–15 minutes).
- Check ears and trim nails roughly every 2–4 weeks.
- Brush teeth daily if possible; at minimum several times a week (about 5 minutes).

## Routine health care
- Monthly flea, tick, and heartworm prevention as directed by a vet.
- Annual vet checkup and vaccinations.

## Warning signs — contact a vet
- Vomiting or diarrhea lasting more than 24 hours, or any blood in either.
- Refusing food for more than a day, or sudden heavy drinking.
- Limping, reluctance to move, or crying out when touched.
- Swollen, hard belly with restlessness or unproductive retching (possible bloat — emergency).
- Lethargy, trouble breathing, or collapse.
