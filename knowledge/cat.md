# Adult Cat Care Guidelines

## Daily activity and enrichment
- Indoor cats need at least two play sessions per day of 10–15 minutes each, using wand toys or chase games that mimic hunting.
- End play with a small meal or treat to complete the "hunt, catch, eat" cycle.
- Provide scratching posts, window perches, and rotate toys weekly to prevent boredom.

## Feeding
- Feed adult cats two or more small meals per day at consistent times; puzzle feeders slow fast eaters.
- Wet food helps hydration; cats often drink too little water on their own.
- Keep fresh water available daily; many cats prefer a fountain or a bowl away from their food.
- Never feed dogs' food, onions, garlic, grapes, or lilies (lilies are highly toxic to cats).

## Litter box
- Scoop litter boxes at least once daily (about 5 minutes).
- Fully change litter and wash the box every 1–2 weeks.
- Provide one box per cat plus one extra, in quiet locations.

## Grooming
- Brush short-haired cats weekly and long-haired cats daily (5–10 minutes) to reduce mats and hairballs.
- Trim nails every 2–3 weeks.

## Routine health care
- Annual vet checkup and vaccinations; flea prevention as directed by a vet.

## Warning signs — contact a vet
- Straining in the litter box or producing little or no urine, especially in males (emergency).
- Not eating for more than 24 hours — cats can develop liver problems quickly.
- Repeated vomiting, hiding more than usual, or sudden weight loss.
- Open-mouth breathing or panting.
