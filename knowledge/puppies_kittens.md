# Puppy and Kitten Care Guidelines

Applies to dogs under about 12 months and cats under about 12 months.

## Feeding
- Puppies and kittens under 6 months need 3–4 small meals a day; move to 2–3 meals between 6 and 12 months.
- Use food labeled for growth ("puppy" or "kitten" formula).
- Keep meal times consistent; this also helps with house training and litter training.
- Fresh water should always be available.

## Exercise and play
- Puppies: a common guideline is about 5 minutes of structured exercise per month of age, up to twice a day (for example, 15 minutes twice a day at 3 months). Avoid long runs or jumping until growth plates close.
- Kittens: several short play sessions a day (5–10 minutes each); they tire quickly and need lots of sleep.

## Training and socialization
- Short training sessions of 5–10 minutes, two or three times a day, work best.
- Puppies need potty breaks after waking, after meals, after play, and every 2–3 hours during the day.
- Gently handle paws, ears, and mouth daily so grooming and vet visits are easier later.
- Introduce new people, sounds, and surfaces calmly during the first few months.

## Health care
- Follow the vet's vaccination and deworming schedule; early visits are usually every 3–4 weeks.
- Discuss spay/neuter timing with a vet.

## Warning signs — contact a vet promptly
- Vomiting or diarrhea, especially with lethargy (young animals dehydrate fast).
- Not eating for more than 12 hours.
- Coughing, nasal discharge, or eye discharge.
- Weakness, pale gums, or a swollen belly.
